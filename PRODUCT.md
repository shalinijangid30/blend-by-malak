# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one funnel:
- **Consumers** — mostly women in Doha, Qatar, who find Blend by Malak via Instagram/TikTok on their phones and want to quickly see this week's workshop, price, and location, then book with one tap. No prior art experience; they're here for a relaxed hangout, not a class.
- **B2B prospects** — cafés, corporate clients, and brand collab leads who land on the site after outreach and judge it as a polished pitch asset, not a booking tool. They care about press credibility, photography quality, and private-event capability.

Booking happens entirely over WhatsApp — the site has no accounts, dashboards, or payment flow. It exists to get a visitor from "curious" to "message sent" as fast as possible on a phone screen.

## Product Purpose

A mobile-first, bilingual (EN/AR) site that turns "DM to book" into a self-serve experience — visible weekly schedule, clear pricing, one-tap WhatsApp booking — while doubling as a pitch asset polished enough to send to cafés, corporate clients, and brand collabs. Originally built as a speculative pre-pitch demo (the built site itself is the outreach hook) before Malak has engaged or supplied final content.

Success looks like: a visitor decides within seconds this is legitimate and fun (not a generic art-class template), finds this week's session, and taps through to WhatsApp — or, for a B2B viewer, comes away convinced this could carry their brand's or café's name credibly.

## Brand Personality

Playful, social, unpretentious. Café-hangout energy, not fine-art or gallery. The tone is "come make a mess with your friends," never "learn proper technique." Palestinian-owned and woman-owned framing is present but tasteful — a fact woven into the story, not a banner shouted at the visitor.

## Anti-references

No generic "art class" stock-template feel — no white-box gallery minimalism, no corporate-agency polish, no stock photography of unrelated hands holding paintbrushes. The real Instagram-native photography and video (café settings, actual workshop mess, real captions) is the asset; lean on it rather than manufactured stock imagery. Avoid anything that reads as a fine-art institution rather than a pop-up hangout.

## Design Principles

- **WhatsApp is the only funnel.** Every CTA resolves to a pre-filled `wa.me` link — no competing booking form, no account creation, nothing that fragments her single inbox.
- **Photography- and video-led, not copy-led.** The work itself is the sell; layouts give real session photos/video room to breathe rather than burying them under chrome.
- **Mobile-first, always.** The entire audience arrives from Instagram/TikTok on a phone; design and test that viewport first, desktop second.
- **Bilingual is structural, not cosmetic.** EN/AR with true RTL mirroring (nav, calendars, layout direction) — not a translated afterthought bolted onto an LTR-only design.
- **Low-friction over feature-complete.** This is a spec/pre-pitch build: prefer a fast, honest, working flow (WhatsApp handoff, simple date/time picker) over building infrastructure (real booking APIs, payments) that outpaces what Malak has agreed to yet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target set. Baseline: semantic HTML, descriptive alt text on all photography, `prefers-reduced-motion` support on the marquee and parallax effects, and keyboard-dismissible (Escape) modals.
